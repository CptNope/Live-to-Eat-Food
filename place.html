Please don't crowd the place cause I wanna go back.

Tina went the month it opened and posted the same week: wood-fired flatbread upstairs, candlepin bowling downstairs, all under one roof. Her verdict was that it's so much fun, and her only worry was that everyone else would find out.

Good for

Dinner with something to do after. Birthdays, groups, a first date that needs an activity, anyone who grew up bowling candlepin.

Things to know

The lanes are downstairs and rented by the hour ($35 an hour when it opened); walk-ins and reservations both work. Wood-fired flatbreads, salads and ice cream upstairs. It's inside The Cove on Green St.
