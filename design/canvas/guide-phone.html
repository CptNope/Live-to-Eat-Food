Where to eat along Shrewsbury Street

Worcester's Restaurant Row runs a mile and a half east from downtown. Start at 72 with pizza and end at 278 with Peruvian and Ecuadorian food in the old Flying Rhino space.

Do not miss out on the haddock or the brussel sprouts.Tina on TikTok, 111 Chop House, Jul 2025
