How Live to Eat Food works

One person's honest food finds around Worcester and Central Mass, turned into places you can find, save and actually go to. Here's what the labels mean and how to reach us.

Restaurants can't buy Tina's opinion.

Tina pays for her meals unless we say otherwise, right on the page. Ads are labeled ads. Being listed is free, forever. Paying never changes what she says.

How Tina picks

She goes, she eats, she posts

Every "Tina ate here" label means she was there. Places she hasn't been are listed and clearly marked.

Honest, even about chains

Her Panda Buffet verdict: an average buffet, and she still goes sometimes. That's the tone.

Nobody buys her opinion

Restaurants can invite her. They can't book a review, and ads are always labeled.

Food events

Pop-ups, openings, festivals and tastings around Central Mass, listed the same way places are: real details, clearly labeled, never paid placement disguised as a pick. Know of one? Send it in.

Tell us your story

Stories is where Tina sits down with the people behind places: the owner who brought candlepin back, the family that kept a lunch car running. Cooks, owners, regulars, all welcome.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or something we got wrong about a place. Corrections to facts are fixed and noted on the page.
