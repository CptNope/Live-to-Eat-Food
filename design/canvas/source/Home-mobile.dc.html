Who's behind the counter at 155 Shrewsbury Street?

The Boulevard Diner was built in 1936 by the Worcester Lunch Car Company, car number 730, and it's one of the few Shrewsbury Street spots Tina hasn't posted from yet. People Behind the Food is where she'd sit down with the families, cooks and bartenders who keep places like this going.
