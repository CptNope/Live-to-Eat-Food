Claim your restaurant

Your basic listing is free and always will be. Claiming it lets you fix your hours, menu and links, and see how many people Tina's pages send to your door. Claiming adds a "Verified business" label. It is never an endorsement, and it never changes what Tina says.

Fix your hours or menu

Wrong hours send people to a locked door. Tell us what changed and we'll update the page and its "last checked" date.

Tell us you're opening

New openings are a big part of what Tina posts about. Tell us early: there's no fee and no promise she'll come, but every one gets read.

Invite Tina

Restaurants can invite her. They can't book a review. If she comes, she says what she thinks, the good and the honest, and the page says if the meal was comped.

Advertise, labeled

Sponsored placements pay for the site. They follow four rules, every time:

  • Every paid placement carries the hatched Sponsored label.
  • Never larger than the Tina pick beside it, and never inside her take, her handwriting or the Sunday list's numbered picks.
  • Sponsor photos are labeled as the sponsor's.
  • Paying never changes what Tina says or where a place ranks.

Work with Live to Eat Food

Events, collaborations, tourism boards, local brands. If it helps people eat better around Central Mass and keeps Tina honest, let's talk.
