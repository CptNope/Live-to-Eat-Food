Candlepin came back to the city that bowled it first.

Candlepin bowling traces back to Worcester in 1880. When Colonial Bowling closed in May 2020, the city went five years without public lanes, until American Flatbread opened ten of them under its dining room at The Cove in May 2025. Stories is where Tina sits down with the people who make places like this happen.
